Community Champion Recognition Awards

Celebrating the unsung heroes who dedicate their time, energy, passion and kindness to improving the lives of others across Cambridgeshire.

Sunday, 5 July 2026
Jesus Green, Cambridge

This year, Cambridge India Day introduces a special new award programme: the Community Champion Recognition Awards.

The awards will be presented on Sunday, 5 July 2026, at Jesus Green, Cambridge, during Cambridge India Day. They aim to recognise and celebrate the unsung heroes who dedicate their time, energy, passion and kindness to improving the lives of others across Cambridgeshire.

Do you know someone who quietly makes a difference in the community? Someone who deserves a simple but meaningful "thank you"?

Please take a few minutes to nominate them. The nomination form is short and only requires some basic information, along with supporting evidence such as links, photographs, or examples of their community work.

Help us shine a light on those who make our communities stronger, kinder and more connected. This will be our gratitude to them.

Who can be nominated?

You can nominate any Cambridgeshire resident, across all communities and regardless of race or nationality, who has carried out some measurable and remarkable work in this region. Nominations require a Gmail account.
