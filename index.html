Why Cambridge India Day?

A cultural bridge in the heart of the city

Cambridge India Day brings India's rich heritage to Jesus Green through exhibitions, dance, music, regional cuisine, arts, crafts and folk culture. It is an open, welcoming event where people of every background come together.

Our vision is to help younger generations of the Indian diaspora stay connected to their roots, while sharing the beauty, diversity and values of India with the wider British, international and global communities that call Cambridge home.

After two successful pilot years at Christ's Pieces, which drew huge crowds even through heavy rain, the festival now moves to the larger setting of Jesus Green. This belongs to all of us.

A cultural bridge

Why Cambridge India Day matters today

Cambridge India Day is more than a festival — it is a cultural bridge between communities. Through food, music, dance, art and regional traditions, it offers an authentic glimpse of one of the world's oldest and most diverse civilisations, while connecting younger British-Indian generations to their roots and helping Cambridge celebrate what makes each of us unique.
