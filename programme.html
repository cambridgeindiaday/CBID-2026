Saturday 4 July 2026 · 11:00 – 12:00

Inauguration Programme

The official opening of Cambridge India Day 2026 — followed by the festival opening to the public at noon.

Saturday, 4 July 2026
11:00 – 12:00
Jesus Green, Cambridge
11:00 am · Arrival of Chief Guests

Reception and welcome of the Lord-Lieutenant, High Sheriff, Mayor, High Commission representative, and our Gold Sponsor.

11:15 – 11:25 am · Traditional Lamp-Lighting Ceremony

Lighting of the ceremonial lamp by the Chief Guests to officially inaugurate Cambridge India Day 2026.

11:25 – 11:30 am · National Anthems
  • National Anthem of the United Kingdom
  • National Anthem of India
11:30 – 11:45 am · Welcome and VIP Addresses
  • Welcome by the Organiser
  • Addresses by the Chief Guests
11:45 am – 12:00 noon · Cultural Performances
  • One cultural performance representing the United Kingdom
  • One cultural performance representing India
12:00 noon onwards · Main Cultural Programme Begins

Dance, music, and cultural performances celebrating the diversity of India's 28 States and 8 Union Territories.

12:00 noon

Festival Opens to the Public

  1. Exhibitions open at the State Pavilions
  2. Food Bazaar Opens
  3. India Bazaar Opens
  4. Sponsor Stalls Open
  5. Main Stage Cultural Programme Begins

Thank you.

This is the inauguration programme for Saturday 4 July. The full running order for Sunday 5 July will be added soon.
