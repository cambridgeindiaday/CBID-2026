What you can sell

  • Indian ethnic clothing — sarees, kurtas, kids' dresses, jewellery
  • Handmade crafts and home décor
  • Bangles, bindis, henna / mehendi, jewellery
  • Spices, pickles, tea, snacks and dry regional food items

  • Live demonstration stalls — pottery, rangoli, calligraphy, handcrafts

What to bring

This is an outdoor event in a Cambridge park. Stallholders provide their own setup — a small or large gazebo, table and chairs. Please come prepared for British summer weather.
